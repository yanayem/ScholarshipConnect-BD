# Privacy Policy for ScholarshipConnectBD

**Last Updated: May 20, 2024**

ScholarshipConnectBD ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting the privacy of our users. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application and web portal.

Please read this privacy policy carefully. If you do not agree with the terms of this privacy policy, please do not access the application.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

We collect information that you provide directly to us when you register, create a profile, or use our services.

### A. Personal Information
- **Account Data**: Name, email address, username, and profile picture.
- **Contact Details**: Phone number and social media links (LinkedIn, GitHub, Facebook, Google Scholar).
- **Identity Verification**: Firebase UID and authentication tokens.

### B. Academic & Professional Information
- **Academic Records**: University name, department, academic level (HSC, Bachelors, etc.), and CGPA.
- **Test Scores**: IELTS and GRE scores.
- **Academic Interests**: Major/Course, research interests, and target countries.
- **Skills & Achievements**: List of technical/soft skills and academic awards.

### C. User-Generated Content
- **Documents**: Certificates, SOPs, CVs, and LORs uploaded to the "Document Vault."
- **Community Data**: Forum posts, comments, polls, stories, and reactions.
- **Messages**: Direct messages between students, mentors, and staff (including shared media).

### D. Usage and Technical Data
- **Device Info**: Device model, OS version, and unique identifiers.
- **FCM Tokens**: Used to send push notifications for deadlines and messages.
- **AI Request Logs**: History of AI tool usage (SOP writing, Eligibility checking).

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for various purposes, including:
- **AI Matchmaking**: Using NLP (TF-IDF & Cosine Similarity) to match your profile with relevant scholarships.
- **Mentorship Sessions**: Facilitating 1-on-1 bookings between students and verified mentors.
- **Scholarship Discovery**: Providing personalized recommendations based on your academic background.
- **Application Tracking**: Managing your saved scholarships and deadline reminders.
- **Gamification**: Managing "ScholarPoints" earned through community contributions.
- **Pro Features**: Verifying subscription status for ScholarConnect Pro tier.

---

## 3. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your data. We share information only in the following cases:
- **With Mentors**: If you book a session, the mentor can see your academic profile to provide guidance.
- **With Admins**: Staff members can review submitted scholarships and moderation reports.
- **AI Service Providers**: Your profile data may be processed by AI models (e.g., Google Gemini or Groq) to generate SOPs or bios.
- **Payment Processor**: Payment data is processed securely via **SSLCommerz**. We do not store your credit card or bKash PIN details.

---

## 4. Data Security & Storage

- **Database**: We use **MongoDB Atlas** with industry-standard encryption for data storage.
- **Media Storage**: Images and documents are stored securely using **Cloudinary** and Django's media handlers.
- **Authentication**: Secured by **Firebase Auth**, ensuring top-tier JWT token verification for every API request.
- **Data Retention**: We retain your personal information as long as your account is active.

---

## 5. Your Privacy Rights

You have the right to:
- **Access & Export**: View or download your data from the Profile section.
- **Update**: Edit your academic and personal information at any time via "Edit Profile."
- **Delete**: Request account deletion by contacting support.
- **Withdraw Consent**: You can disable push notifications or location access (if any) via device settings.

---

## 6. Third-Party Services

Our app integrates with the following third-party services:
- **Firebase** (Google): For authentication and push notifications.
- **SSLCommerz**: For secure payment processing in Bangladesh.
- **Cloudinary**: For hosting user-uploaded images.
- **Google Scholar**: For academic publication integration.
